# Sacred-Call
## an Exploration and Adventure Modpack

This modpack focuses on the point of exploration and adventure.

a brief overview of the Mods in this Modpack and what they do:

- Origin Mod: lets you choose your path with its pros and cons
- Craftify: lets you control your spotify within Minecraft
- Lootr: instances the chests for every player
- Croptopia: adds more crops, tress and food
